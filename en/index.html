The studio

Big Art Gallery

Big Art Gallery started from the idea that a tattoo is first of all a work of art: studied on the person, drawn by hand, and put on skin with the same care given to a piece on a wall. We work by appointment, one project at a time, so every drawing gets the time it asks for.
